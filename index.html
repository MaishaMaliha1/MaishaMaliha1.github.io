Teaching

CS1213: Programming with Python

I am currently serving as the primary instructor for CS1213: Programming with Python at the University of Oklahoma in Fall 2025.

Previously, I worked as a lab instructor for the same course in Fall 2024 and Spring 2025, where I led weekly lab sessions, supported students with hands-on Python practice, and helped build their confidence in core programming concepts.

Horizon Mini-College: Computer Science Teaching

I have also taught computer science as part of OU’s Horizon Mini-College program, designing and delivering age-appropriate lessons in computing for students from 1st through 8th grade.

Through this outreach, I aim to make programming approachable and enjoyable for younger students, inspiring their curiosity for problem solving and computational thinking.

Research & Accomplishments

Research Focus

My research lies at the intersection of Explainable AI, Reinforcement Learning, and Cybersecurity. I work on designing methods that make learning systems more transparent, trustworthy, and robust in safety-critical environments.

I am particularly interested in mask-based explanations, multi-agent collaboration, and RL-driven intrusion detection.

Recent Highlights
  • 2025 NeurIPS 2025 Workshop (FoRLM) submission: “Hessian-Enhanced Token Attribution (HETA): Interpreting Autoregressive Language Models”, co-authored with Vishal Pramanik and Sumit Kumar Jha.
  • 2025 Research on MAGIC-MASK, a framework for multi-agent collaboration with mask-based explainability in reinforcement learning.
  • 2024 First and Second Place awards at NAECON 2024 for poster presentations on aviation analytics and explainable AI.
